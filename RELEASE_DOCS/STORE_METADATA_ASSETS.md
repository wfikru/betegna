### 3.3 App Icon & Logo

**Specifications:**
- **Size:** 512 × 512 pixels (1024 × 1024 for submission)
- **Format:** PNG or JPEG
- **Background:** Opaque (no transparency for some stores)
- **Rounded Corners:** Let stores handle rounding
- **Text in Icon:** Avoid (doesn't scale well)
- **Safe Area:** Keep important elements in center 80%

**Design Recommendations:**
- High contrast and recognizable at small sizes
- Avoid busy patterns or too many colors
- Should work in grayscale
- Should be unique and not similar to competitors

## 9. RATING & REVIEW STRATEGY

### 9.1 How to Encourage Positive Reviews

1. **In-App Prompts (After successful task completion)**
   - "How was your experience? Leave a review!"
   - Prompt users to rate app after 3+ successful tasks

2. **Support Quality**
   - Respond quickly to support requests
   - Fix bugs promptly and release updates
   - Monitor reviews and address concerns

3. **Feature Updates**
   - Regular updates with new features (monthly goal)
   - Notify users of improvements
   - Ask for feedback in release notes

### 9.2 Managing Negative Reviews

- Read all reviews carefully
- Respond professionally to complaints
- Offer support and solutions
- Address recurring issues in updates
- Track review sentiment over time
